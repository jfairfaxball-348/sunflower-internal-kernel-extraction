1. Mathematical programme

The permanent programme's headline objective is the Erdős–Rado Sunflower Conjecture.

The selected first-paper research direction is:

Quantitative Internal-Kernel Sunflower Extraction

This follows the open question identified in Section 6 of:

Janzer, Jin, Sudakov and Wu, Sunflowers and Ramsey Problems for Restricted Intersections, Combinatorica (2026).

The proposed theorem concerns a finite k-uniform set family

[
\mathcal F\subseteq\binom{[n]}{k}
]

and an integer m\geq2.

The objective is to establish a constant c_k>0, independent of m, n and \mathcal F, such that there exists

[
\mathcal F'\subseteq\mathcal F
]

with

[
|\mathcal F'|\geq c_km^{-k}|\mathcal F|
]

and with the following property:

For every two distinct members A,B\in\mathcal F', their intersection A\cap B is the kernel of an m-petal sunflower whose members all belong to \mathcal F'.

The distinction between witnesses in \mathcal F and witnesses in \mathcal F' is essential.

This is a proposed research target, not an established new theorem.
