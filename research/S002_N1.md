## Frozen statement and equivalent formulation

For m>=3, each finite F subset binom([n],3), lexicographically fixed maximum residual matchings for each proper K define psi(K), empty if matching >=m. Independent uniform vertex colours chi:[n]->[288m]; good edges are rainbow and avoid all colours of psi(K) outside themselves. For P a three-colour palette, G_P consists of good edges with palette P. Let b_m(G) be the maximum number of edges in a subfamily H subset G whose every distinct pair's exact intersection K has residual matching number nu(H(K))>=m; define b_m(empty)=0.

L_AVG asks for absolute eta>0 with E_chi sum_P b_m(G_P(chi))>=eta|F| for all m,n,F. For a *fixed* colouring, sum_P b_m(G_P) is precisely the maximum cardinality of a colour-palette-respecting selection H of good edges such that **each palette slice** H intersect G_P is internally m-valid. This is a partitioned objective: the union over P need **not** be internally valid. The expectation averages optimal cardinalities after observing chi; interchanging max and expectation or replacing b_m(G_P) by |G_P| is invalid without proof. A weighted version replacing equal unit edge rewards by nonnegative weights would strictly extend the frozen claim; it has NOT been inferred. This is sufficient for R3 via selecting one palette, not a necessary equivalence.
